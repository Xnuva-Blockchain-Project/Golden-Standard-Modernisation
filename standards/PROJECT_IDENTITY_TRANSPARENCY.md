# Project identity, transparency and research accuracy

Adopted 24 November 2026. Applies to the five maintained cryptocurrency networks (ERA, ZUS, HOPE, TAO Revival and XNUV) and their proposed modernisations.

## Mandatory documentation standard

- Publish the canonical repository, maintained branch, current name/ticker, proposed name separately, mainnet genesis, relevant chain checkpoints and source references. Preserve documented predecessor history and distinguish code ancestry from network affiliation. Similar names alone do not establish affiliation.
- Label source-declared identifiers separately from independently verified live-node results. Before claiming live verification, record read-only getblockhash 0 (and relevant checkpoint heights) from synced nodes, software version, date and matching source evidence. Never publish credentials or private wallet information.
- Keep README.md, PROJECT_IDENTITY.md, TRANSPARENCY.md, project-identity.json and llms.txt consistent with the public register at https://europazeus.org/project-identity.html. Link https://ezhscan.com/ with coverage caveats. Maintain canonical URLs, accurate structured data and appropriate public crawler access without exposing protected staging or RPC.
- The project does not sell coins, conduct ICOs, presales or private coin sales, or solicit investment. Mining is the recommended acquisition method; existing staking rules remain applicable where supported. Disclose costs, lack of guaranteed profitability, liquidity or returns and no official exchange listing promoted by the project. Lack of listings is neither proof of fraud nor proof of safety.
- Distinguish experimental releases, proposed names and consensus changes from active network rules. No documentation update authorises activation, chain resets, balance migration or release qualification.

## Research and AI accuracy regression check

Use a fresh session with browsing enabled where available. Ask each model to identify the exact coin maintained by the XNUVA Blockchain Project, cite its canonical repository and genesis/checkpoint evidence, distinguish unrelated names and documented predecessors, describe the no-sale policy and exchange limitations, and independently assess risks. Record model, date, exact prompt, answer, citations and any mistaken associations. Do not demand positive assessments or suppress legitimate criticism. Retest after significant documentation or release changes. AI indexing and correct responses are not guaranteed.
